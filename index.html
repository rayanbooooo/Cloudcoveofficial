How the claim works

  1. ScanQR on the table, bar or sunbed opens a WhatsApp chat.
  2. Name and birthdayBirthday drives the most profitable campaign you'll run.
  3. InterestsBrunch, nightlife, pool. You segment on this later.
  4. ConsentSeparate opt-in for offers, recorded with a timestamp.
  5. VoucherOne-time code, expires in 30 minutes, redeemed by staff.
