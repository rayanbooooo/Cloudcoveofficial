The method

Three habits that separate traders from gamblers.

01

Structure

Learn to read the market the same way every day. One framework, clear criteria, no improvising on a feeling.

02

Risk

Decide what you can lose before you enter. Position sizing and stop discipline come before any entry idea.

03

Review

Every trade goes in a journal and gets reviewed. Mistakes become rules, and rules become habits.

We don't sell certainty, because there isn't any. We teach a way of working that survives being wrong.

Do I need experience?

No. The playbook starts from the basics. If you already trade, jump straight to the risk and review modules.

Do you give signals or guarantee profits?

No. This is education. Nobody can guarantee results in trading, and most people lose money. The goal is to build a disciplined process and manage risk.

How much money do I need?

You can learn and paper trade with no capital. Only ever trade with money you can afford to lose.
